PearNotes — brand identity proposals

Assignment E3 · visual design exploration. Five meaningfully different marks generated via FLORA (Recraft V4.1 Pro Vector + GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst). Nothing here replaces installed brand assets — pick a direction and final exports get produced in the follow-up task.

Design grammar: die-cut sticker / scrapbook materiality · ink #0B0B0D · warm paper #F5EFDF · restrained lime #C6F750 · wordmark set in Bricolage Grotesque 800 (OFL, already the project display font).
FLORA project: prj_ns74pm82b56q9annhw5zjxt1e18hcd8d — “PearNotes E3 Brand Identity” · open canvas

1 · The Folded Pear pear + note Recraft V4.1 Pro Vector

The most literal reading of the name: a pear whose bottom-right edge curls up like a folded sticky-note, revealing a lime underside. The fold does double duty — it says paper and it says there's something underneath / a reveal, which maps nicely onto “notes transforming into understanding.” Strong, single-gesture silhouette; reads instantly at favicon size; closest to the product name for memorability.

2 · The Open Page knowledge motif Recraft V4.1 Pro Vector

An open book whose pages carry a sprouting stem-and-leaf: notes literally growing into understanding. The most “learning-forward” mark — communicates education without any fruit at all, so it won't be confused with pear/food brands. Bookish, calm, trustworthy; the sprout keeps it playful. Very clean geometry — arguably the strongest at 16px.

3 · Pip, the Note-Buddy character GPT 2.5 Sunburst + vector sketch

An original die-cut paper-pear character: a little folded-paper pear standing on a curled sheet of notes, pencil in hand. Maximum personality and approachability — the “study companion” direction. Distinctly not an owl (different silhouette, no mascot-suit proportions), so no Duolingo adjacency. Trade-off: character marks are busiest at favicon size — the face+paper base needs simplification for 16px, and the final asset would be redrawn as vector.

Total generation spend: $3.82 (round 1: $2.325 · round 2 Folded-P refinement: $1.495). Method: text-to-image / text-to-vector prompts (no image references), full prompts preserved in PROPOSAL.md. Variants (dark, monochrome, transparent) were produced by remapping fills on the returned SVG path data — no additional model calls.

Rights: outputs are original generations, not derived from OurSpaces or third-party artwork. Recraft and GPT Image terms grant the workspace user rights to generated outputs, but AI-generated marks may have limited copyright/trademark registrability — recommend a human designer cleanup + trademark search (esp. vs. Pear/Pear Deck/Notion/Duolingo) before the mark is registered or shipped. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans are SIL OFL — safe for logo typesetting; the wordmark should be converted to outlines in final exports.
