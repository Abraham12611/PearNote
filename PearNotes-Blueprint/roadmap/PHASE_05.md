# Phase 05 — Complete multiplayer and group workspaces

**Goal:** support rich, persistent learning communities built with the original OurSpaces canvas metaphor.

## 05.1 General HQ

- Group creation, owner/mod/member roles, invite approval, announcements, calendar, files, chat, recent activity and room navigation.
- Editable layout with group identity, avatars and decorative artifacts.

**Exit:** a group can organize without leaving PearNotes.

## 05.2 Study Rooms

- Shared lesson references from explicit source sharing, co-annotations, follow-presenter camera (opt-in), shared quiz surfaces with personal answers.
- Peer cursors, collision-safe object moving, voice presence later if justified.

**Exit:** multiple members complete a collaborative study session with consistent shared objects and separate individual progress.

## 05.3 Brainstorm and project templates

- Brainstorm: notes, clustering, idea links, voting, AI proposals, keepers.
- Project: tasks, timelines, responsibilities, sources, milestone and deliverable cards.

**Exit:** templates create valid functioning spaces, not decorative screenshots.

## 05.4 Revision Arena and learning games

- Cooperative challenges, knowledge relay, timed rounds with accessible alternatives, group trophy hunts and optional rankings.
- Score integrity with server-authoritative attempts and protection against chat leaks.

**Exit:** complete round with recoverable state and fair result reveal.

## 05.5 Moderation and permission maturity

- Role-based editing, invite expiry, blocking/reporting, member removal, group archive/ownership transfer, AI scope control.

**Exit:** permission test suite demonstrates private/public boundary under all roles.
