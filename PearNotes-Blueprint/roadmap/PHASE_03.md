# Phase 03 — Advanced learning intelligence

**Goal:** move from competent adaptive lessons to a well-grounded, inspectable personal knowledge model.

## 03.1 Versioned concept graph

- Expand `concepts`, prerequisite/related/application edges, examples and source references.
- Validate candidate relationships against curriculum and sample educator reviews; detect cycles and contradictory material.
- Visual graph explorer shows authored and AI-proposed links distinctly.

**Exit:** trace any assessed objective to source segments and related prerequisites.

## 03.2 Mastery evidence calibration

- Require independent recall/application evidence and structured rubric outcomes.
- Create evidence weights for hints, repeat attempts, and delayed success with documented calibration.
- Add an appeals/regrade trail for questionable AI grading.

**Exit:** consistency benchmarks show acceptable agreement with human-reviewed answers; uncertain cases don't silently become mastery.

## 03.3 Adaptive path replanning

- Recommend skipping redundant basics when prior knowledge is demonstrated; propose new optional branches for actual gaps.
- Show user-readable explanation of the recommendation and preview changes. Never silently reorder authored curriculum.
- Preserve historical versions, links and attempts.

**Exit:** different users receive appropriately different recommendations from same sources, and can inspect rationale.

## 03.4 Revision and retention

- Scheduled retrieval practice, configurable exam deadlines and short study slots.
- Longitudinal progress with concept history, confusion tracking and delayed recall outcomes.
- Rest-day/streak-friendly revision reminders; avoid burdening users with AI-generated quotas.

**Exit:** learner returns after several days and sees relevant practice, not just the last-opened lesson.

## 03.5 Advanced Tutor pedagogy

- Socratic questioning, comparison of approaches, step-by-step worked-example review, oral exam mode when audio arrives.
- Controlled `createActivity`/`annotate` actions and provenance; approval/audit/undo required.

**Exit:** tutor demonstrably helps overcome a gap without doing every graded activity for the learner.
