# Phase 06 — Multimodal input and specialized educational worlds

**Goal:** transform heterogeneous legitimate educational assets into suitably interactive activities.

## 06.1 DOCX, PPTX, image and page adapters

- Parse original structure, slides, speaker notes, figures, tables, line/page references.
- OCR for image-only docs with confidence and correction interface.

**Exit:** every generated claim routes to an addressable source segment or explicit supplemental reference.

## 06.2 Audio and voice

- Record/upload lecture/voice note; transcription with timestamps and available speaker metadata, corrections, transcript-backed lesson building.
- Oral Teach-Back, language pronunciation where reliable and legally suitable.

**Exit:** audio lesson output matches original recording and source timestamps.

## 06.3 Video and permitted web media

- Authorized MP4 clips and transcripts; use selective keyframe understanding, web research/reference capture with source URLs.
- YouTube captions only through permitted access; user consent and copyrighted content controls.

**Exit:** extracted video concepts link to actual clip time, no fabricated observation.

## 06.4 Interactive visual environments

- SAM-backed region selection where useful; diagram labeler, formula/graph explorer, anatomy and chemistry visual objects.
- Validate all science diagrams; fallback to curated assets if image model uncertain.

**Exit:** at least one accurate interactive diagram used for real assessment.

## 06.5 Deterministic simulations

- Subject plugins for physics motion, probability, geometry, circuit basics, biology visualizations and safe medical case simulations.
- AI may select/configure explanations; verified code and equations govern simulation behavior.

**Exit:** simulations reproduce trusted reference outputs and work without model calls at every interaction.

## 06.6 Widget packs by discipline

- Science, Mathematics, Medicine/Pharmacy, History, Language and Computer Science packs; curated templates and safety review.

**Exit:** AI lesson composer selects widgets based on objectives, not always same four cards.
