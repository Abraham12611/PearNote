# Phase 08 — Ecosystem, distribution and research

**Goal:** a sustainable platform for student-created, educator-reviewed and institution-managed learning worlds.

## 08.1 Educator authoring

- Teachers edit AI curriculum paths, build/approve assessments, curate source packs and publish to groups.
- Moderated versioning and verified answer keys; teacher overrides of questionable feedback.

**Exit:** educator can review and deliver one complete course unit.

## 08.2 Classes and institutions

- Courses, enrollment, assignment deadlines, class calendar, learner admin and appropriately privacy-preserving progress reports.
- Institution roles, retention rules and regulatory review before involving minors.

**Exit:** one pilot institution can run a class without cross-student data leaks.

## 08.3 Creator marketplace

- Template/learning-world pack publishing, discoverability, quality review, attribution, license and version control, optional revenue share.
- Provenance for imported published curricula and dispute/takedown system.

**Exit:** authors distribute reusable, verified lesson packs with license clarity.

## 08.4 Integrations and portability

- Authorized calendar, cloud-storage, LMS, research and publisher connections; import/export to open formats where practical.
- Student retains portable learning sources and notes; interoperability doesn't bypass access boundaries.

**Exit:** controlled import/export and consent flows pass integration tests.

## 08.5 Growth and learning-effectiveness research

- Invite/referral loops based on sharing meaningful artifacts, not XP spam.
- Run voluntary, ethically designed comparisons of spatial lessons vs standard materials on delayed recall and transfer tests.

**Exit:** retention and educational outcomes support continued investment in new mechanics.
