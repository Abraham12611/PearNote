# Phase 04 — Gamification, mastery artifacts and collectability

**Goal:** make progress feel alive while keeping rewards subordinate to real learning.

## 04.1 Reliable XP economy

- Expand eligible server-validated events; implement rule versions, caps and abuse prevention.
- XP display on header/profile, consistent across devices, backfilled from immutable ledger.

**Exit:** duplicated attempts/retries cannot farm currency.

## 04.2 Flexible streaks & quests

- Qualified-day rules, weekly learning goals, time-zone handling, rest/recovery days.
- Daily/weekly quests tied to useful learning events, not endless clicks.

**Exit:** quest completion maps to real attempt evidence.

## 04.3 Trophy and collectible library

- Design dozens of original illustrated science/math/language awards, profile frames and themed canvas artifacts.
- Trophy shelf as tangible scrapbook; details show achievement evidence; private/public visibility control.

**Exit:** unlock and place an object in Home, with accessible non-spatial summary.

## 04.4 Spatial trophy hunts

- Optional hidden knowledge chests, collectible concept cards and discovery seals attached to real objectives.
- Unlock requires a valid practice/explanation task. No random answer harvesting or secret objective only solvable by model guessing.

**Exit:** one end-to-end treasure route is educational and enjoyable in user tests.

## 04.5 Social competition (opt-in)

- Friends-only or cohort-based leagues, short seasons, group quests, contribution recognition.
- Privacy settings, anti-cheat, equitable participation and alternative noncompetitive achievements.

**Exit:** players can opt out without losing core study access or being shamed.

## 04.6 Reward analytics

- Inspect retention, delayed recall and burnout indicators; revise reward economy against these, not just engagement.

**Exit:** measured benefit or controlled sunset of ineffective mechanics.
