# Product vision, positioning, and non-negotiable principles

## One-sentence promise

**PearNotes turns scattered study material into a living learning world: AI constructs a grounded learning journey, students explore and practice in tactile lesson spaces, and the world evolves as their understanding develops.**

## The problem beneath the problem

Documents, videos, voice notes, slides and shared links are **content containers**, not environments for forming durable understanding. Existing AI study apps frequently stop at summarization, flashcards, or a chat interface. Standard collaborative tools preserve messages but do not distinguish conversation from learning evidence. PearNotes asks a different question: *What if learning resources became a persistent place where people could manipulate concepts, struggle, create artifacts, remember where they left off, and learn with friends?*

## Product pillars

1. **Creation from reality:** no default subjects. Learning worlds originate from student sources or deliberate manual creation; suggested names and splits are editable.
2. **Guided freedom:** the path drawer gives orientation like a progression game, while lessons open onto flexible spatial boards. Guided/Explore modes coexist.
3. **Learning with evidence:** activities are tied to objectives, objectives to concepts, concepts to source passages. Time spent ≠ demonstrated mastery.
4. **Worlds with memory:** learned concepts, notes, corrected errors, notes promoted from discussions, revisions and trophies persist.
5. **Social, not coercively social:** study alone or invite friends. Group collaboration never discloses personal mastery and private sources by default.
6. **AI as facilitator:** tutor has context and controlled tools, not a giant ungrounded chat box. It may recommend or add reviewed activities; humans direct the room.
7. **Creative and credible:** colorful, tactile, responsive UI with validated AI outputs and honest uncertainty.

## Audience

**First wedge:** independent secondary-school/university students with PDF/text materials needing a memorable way to study—demonstrated with cell biology. **Secondary:** self-learners, exam-prep study groups, university project teams. **Long-term:** educators and organizations. See [personas](PERSONAS.md).

## Product language

- **Home:** personal landing world and navigation.
- **Subject:** owner-controlled collection of learning sources and curriculum.
- **Learning Path:** sections → units → lessons, represented as an illustrated navigable map.
- **Lesson Space:** persistent canvas showing a single lesson's activities, sources and user artifacts.
- **Group:** membership and roles; contains a **General HQ** and purpose rooms.
- **Room:** shared canvas for studying, brainstorming, projects or revision.
- **Pear Tutor:** personal AI facilitator with access only to authorized student context.
- **Learning artifact:** a note, explanation, concept connection, answer, annotation or kept thread produced by a human.

## What makes PearNotes defensible

- **Not** PDF→summary→quiz.
- **Not** cosmetic 3D avatars over a standard LMS.
- **Not** a Duolingo path pasted into a whiteboard.
- The central product transformation is **unstructured source → graph of learnable concepts → validated path and spatial lesson objects → observable learning evidence → controlled environmental adaptation**.
- Meaningful unlocks and trophies are spatial representations of achieved understanding, not merely points.

## Explicit anti-goals

- Don't promise verified credentials or clinical accuracy; educational support only.
- Don't fabricate missing textbook claims, sources, exam performance or scientific diagrams.
- Don't punish breaks in a way that undermines wellbeing.
- Don't let an AI silently reorder/delete user work or publish private materials.
- Don't require costly 3D rendering: illustrated 2D/2.5D assets and SVG paths are first choice.
- Don't build all subjects and widget disciplines before a reliable Biology slice.

## Core hypothesis and falsifiability

If a student's own materials are rendered as a connected, interactive world, they will find it easier to begin, navigate and return to learning, and focused activity/feedback will improve observed understanding. This is a **hypothesis**, not a proven claim. Evaluate activation, valid assessment outcomes, delayed recall, retention, accessibility and cost (see [metrics](METRICS.md)).
